AggieRide: Mobility Powered by Travel Intent

Every day, thousands of Aggies in College Station and Bryan head to the exact same places: Walmart, H-E-B, Target, campus buildings, and campus events. AggieRide replaces transactional ride-hailing with a community mobility platform centered on travel intent.

lightbulb Why AggieRide is NOT an Uber Clone

close Traditional Ride Apps

  • • Driver lists route; riders search blindly
  • • Transactional surge pricing ($18-25 for 3 miles)
  • • Riding with unverified strangers
  • • Individual door-to-door suburban delays

check The AggieRide Paradigm

  • • Students express travel intent ("Heading to Walmart Sat 11 AM")
  • • Automated destination clusters with 4 live community metrics
  • • Student drivers offer seats to existing demand
  • • 7 predefined campus pickup hubs eliminate detours
  • • 100% verified Aggies with directory OTP check

Journey 1 • Passenger

Express Travel Intent & Form Destination Groups

Carlos enters his destination (Walmart), preferred time (Saturday 11 AM), $\pm 1$ hour flexibility window, and pickup area (Park West). AggieRide instantly clusters him into an active group with 4 live metrics: Interested Aggies, Looking for driver, Offered seats, and Group Status.

Intent Input Form 4 Community Metrics Group Formation Subtle Feedback
Journey 2 • Driver

Driver Discovers Unmet Demand & Offers Ride

Maya logs in as a verified driver with her 2022 Honda Civic. The app highlights an unmet student demand banner: "6 Aggies want to go to Walmart Saturday morning. Heading there?". Maya taps to offer 3 seats to the cluster.

Role Isolation (Drivers Only) Demand Alert Banner Instant Seat Offering
Journey 3 • Coordination

Seat Request & Real-time Acceptance

Carlos requests a seat in Maya's ride from the Park West pickup hub. Maya receives the request in her "Offered Rides" management tab, reviews Carlos's student profile, and approves the seat. Remaining seats decrement automatically.

Passenger Request Driver Approval Flow Dynamic Capacity Update
Journey 4 • Logistics

7 Shared Predefined Pickup Hubs

College Station apartment complexes have complicated security gates and speed bumps. AggieRide replaces chaotic door-to-door pickups with 7 designated, safe student hubs: MSC, Northgate, Park West, White Creek, The Gardens, Univ Apts, and Century Square.

Interactive Hub Map Walking Distance Estimator Zero Suburban Detours
Journey 5 • Safety

Boarding OTP & Student Safety Verification

Before boarding, Carlos views his official Digital Boarding Pass with a cryptographic 6-digit OTP code (AG-7193). Passengers present this code to the driver to prevent wrong-car boarding. All users require verified @tamu.edu credentials.

Boarding Pass & OTP (AG-7193) TAMU Directory Verification Zero Stranger Risk
Journey 6 • Communication

Private Contextual Coordination Messaging

In-app direct messaging allows Carlos and Maya to coordinate exact arrival timing at the Park West Tower 1 Circle without sharing personal phone numbers. The ride route and status remain pinned at the top of the chat.

In-App Chat Pinned Ride Banner Privacy Preserved

Aggies can discover and join carpool groups for campus events like the TAMU Makeathon at Zachry Complex, Kyle Field Game Day, Midnight Yell, and First Friday Bryan, arriving together as a group.
